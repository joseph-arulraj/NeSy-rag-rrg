# Knowledge base (v2/kg/) — what it contains, where each part comes from, what is unreviewed
Updated 2026-10-07. Loaded and validated by `nesy/kg.py` (schema, unique ids, references, acyclic is_a and
part_of; RadLex map and device table). Plain YAML interpreted in Python (no triple store, Prolog or LTN).
Every row has a `source` field (`scripts/add_sources.py`; counts in RESULTS.md). Rows whose source is
"author-curated, needs review", every `approximate` or `none` RadLex match, every glossary entry and every
external mapping still **need radiologist review**.

Findings from the knowledge-base work (details in RESULTS.md):
- RadLex has no class for cardiomegaly, enlarged cardiomediastinum, pleural thickening, sternotomy wires or CABG grafts; it does have the six sided lung zones (RID1349–1356), previously treated as missing.
- Our finding hierarchy (CheXpert, appearance-based: lung opacity as the umbrella) and RadLex's (mechanism-based: collapse, displaced substance, respiratory disorder) disagree on every comparable finding edge; device nesting agrees for all 13 comparable types. Our hierarchy is unchanged.
- Reports and belief graphs state zone and side, never lobe (rules G2, P8).

## Glossary-derived concepts (task 7, ablation only)
`data/concepts/glossary_phrases.csv`: 52 short phrases written from Fleischner glossary terms (term and page per row; our wording). 31 pass the 0.60 fit-split AUROC floor (`data/concepts/concepts_glossary.csv`, embeddings `data/concepts/glossary_embeddings.pt`). A head on them matches the C+A+R head (see RESULTS.md); not used in the pipeline. Phrases need radiologist review if ever adopted.
